## Q2 2026

Q2 had some really good stuff. I still haven't had enough time to go through everything that came out, but there were plenty of releases that stayed in rotation for me.

As usual, doom, death-doom and death metal took most of my attention. There was also some sludge, stoner doom, black metal and other filthy stuff in there. These are my Top 10 favourites from April to June, followed by four special mentions.

### 1. Draconian - In Somnolent Ruin

* Personal rating: 10/10
* Genre: Gothic Death-Doom

![Draconian - In Somnolent Ruin](/img/2026Q2/in-somnolent-ruin.jpg)

Draconian absolutely nailed this one. Massive death-doom riffs, deep growls, beautiful clean vocals and that cold gothic atmosphere all over the place. This is pretty much the kind of death-doom I always want to hear.

The contrast between the heavy riffs and the clean vocals works so fucking well. Some of the melodies are ridiculously beautiful and then the growls and crushing guitars come in and completely drag everything back into the darkness.

I played the shit out of this album during the quarter. Dark, heavy, melancholic and ridiculously atmospheric. Easily my favourite release of Q2.

---

### 2. Warning - Rituals of Shame

* Personal rating: 10/10
* Genre: Doom Metal
  
![Warning - Rituals of Shame](/img/2026Q2/rituals-of-shame.jpg)

Warning coming back after 20 years and sounding this miserable was not something I expected. Slow, heavy and depressing riffs with Patrick Walker's vocals sitting right on top of everything. There is so much emotion in the guitars without needing a million different things happening.

This album completely fucking consumed me. I spent a lot of late nights listening to this one. The songs are long and slow but never felt boring to me. Everything just keeps dragging you deeper into that miserable atmosphere.

It has that very specific kind of sadness that doom does best. Fucking beautiful and depressing album.

---

### 3. Fires in the Distance - Circadian Promise

* Personal rating: 9/10
* Genre: Melodic Death-Doom

![Fires in the Distance - Circadian Promise](/img/2026Q2/circadian-promise.jpg)

I was waiting for this one after Air Not Meant For Us, and it ended up being another great release from Fires in the Distance. Huge melodic guitars, harsh vocals, synths and those slow heavy sections all work really well together.

It doesn't hit me exactly the same way as the previous album, but there is still a lot here that I loved. Some of the melodies are fucking beautiful and I kept replaying certain sections just for the guitars.

There is something about the way this band mixes melody with death-doom that just works for me. Heavy without losing the emotional side of it.

---


### 4. Hubris - Acts of Sedition

* Personal rating: 8.5/10
* Genre: Black Metal

![Hubris - Acts of Sedition](/img/2026Q2/acts-of-sedition.jpg)

This one caught me off guard. Really nasty black metal with dark riffs, aggressive vocals and an ugly atmosphere that just works.

There is something very raw about the way this album sounds. It doesn't feel overly clean or polished and that made it hit much harder for me. The riffs have enough going on without turning into a fucking mess.

I kept finding myself putting this back on during the quarter. It didn't take long for it to become one of my favourite black metal releases of Q2.**

---

### 5. Immolation - Descent

* Personal rating: 8/10
* Genre: Death Metal

![Immolation - Descent](/img/2026Q2/descent.jpg)

Immolation being Immolation. Filthy riffs, weird guitar work, disgusting vocals and that twisted atmosphere they somehow always manage to create. Their guitars just sound fucking wrong in the best possible way.

This is the kind of death metal I can put on when I just want riffs. I don't need anything else. Just that nasty Immolation sound hitting again and again. Some of the guitar work on this album is absolutely fucking ridiculous. It feels chaotic as hell but there is still so much control underneath it.

---

### 6. Sectarian Defacement - Hostile Consuming Rapture

* Personal rating: 7.5/10
* Genre: Brutal Death Metal / Grindcore

![Sectarian Defacement - Hostile Consuming Rapture](/img/2026Q2/hostile-consuming-rapture.jpg)

This shit slaps! Absolutely disgusting album.

The guitars sound fucking rotten, the vocals are ridiculously deep and the whole thing feels like being hit by a truck made of broken bones. Everything is so aggressive and dirty that there is barely any room to breathe once it gets going.

I love death metal when it gets this filthy. No unnecessary bullshit, just brutal riffs, disgusting vocals and enough blast beats to destroy whatever is left of your brain.

---

### 7. Onchocerciasis Esophagogastroduodenoscopy - Fugue Gnawed from the Scabbed God Cerebrum

* Personal rating: 7.5/10
* Genre: Slamming Brutal Death Metal

![Onchocerciasis Esophagogastroduodenoscopy - Fugue Gnawed from the Scabbed God Cerebrum](/img/2026Q2/fugue-gnawed.jpg)

The album title alone should tell you what you're getting into.

This is some seriously disgusting shit. Guttural vocals, slam riffs, weird technical parts and an absolutely ridiculous amount of heaviness packed into the whole thing. It sounds completely fucked up and that is exactly why I enjoyed it.

Not something I would throw on every day, but whenever I wanted something completely filthy and stupidly heavy, this worked perfectly.

Just pure fucking violence.

---

### 8. Ordh - Blind in Abyssal Realms

* Personal rating: 7.5/10
* Genre: Progressive Death Metal

![Ordh - Blind in Abyssal Realms](/img/2026Q2/blind-in-abyssal-realms.jpg)

This one caught me pretty quickly. Dark progressive death metal with a lot going on in the guitars without losing that filthy death metal feeling. There are some really interesting riffs and melodies throughout the album and it never feels completely predictable.

I kept going back to this because it has that dirty underground feeling I always look for in death metal. There is enough weird shit happening in the guitars to keep things interesting too. Really fucking solid debut.

---

### 9. Monolord - Neverending

* Personal rating: 7/10
* Genre: Doom Metal / Stoner Metal

![Monolord - Neverending](/img/2026Q2/neverending.jpg)

Monolord doing huge riffs again. Big fuzzy guitars, slow drums and that massive wall of sound that makes everything feel heavier than it actually is. They also went for shorter songs this time, which actually worked really well for me.

I kept coming back to this one because it is just fun to listen to. There are some really good riffs throughout the album and *It's Neverending* with Jörgen Sandström on vocals is fucking massive. Monolord still know how to make a simple riff sound enormous.

---

### 10. Corrosion of Conformity - Good God / Baad Man

* Personal rating: 7/10
* Genre: Sludge / Stoner / Southern Metal

![Corrosion of Conformity - Good God / Baad Man](/img/2026Q2/good-god-baad-man.jpg)

This was a really easy album to get into. C.O.C. came back with a huge double album full of dirty guitars, southern rock, sludge, stoner riffs and plenty of that classic C.O.C. sound. There is a lot of music here, but I never really felt like I had to sit through it.

I especially liked the heavier stuff. *You Or Me*, *Run For Your Life* and *Asleep On The Killing Floor* were some of the tracks I kept coming back to. And *Forever Amplified* has that slow doom feeling towards the end which I absolutely loved. Really solid comeback.

---

## Special Mentions

### Flykt - Sinister Strain

* Personal rating: 7/10
* Genre: Black Metal
* Release date: June 19, 2026

![Flykt - Sinister Strain](/img/2026Q2/sinister-strain.jpg)

Cold and aggressive black metal with some really good melodic guitar work. The riffs are sharp and the whole album has that nasty underground feeling.

Some of the longer tracks worked especially well for me. Not everything hit equally hard, but there was enough good stuff here that I kept coming back to it.

---

### Bewitched - Diabolical Death Mass

* Personal rating: 6/10
* Genre: Black Metal / Thrash Metal
* Release date: April 24, 2026

![Bewitched - Diabolical Death Mass](/img/2026Q2/diabolical-death-mass.jpg)

Old-school black/thrash that sounds exactly as evil as it should. Fast riffs, nasty vocals and plenty of filthy 80s/90s metal energy.

Nothing complicated here. Just riffs, speed and evil shit. Sometimes that's all I fucking need.

---

### Bodysnatcher - Hell Is Here, Hell Is Home

* Personal rating: 6/10
* Genre: Deathcore
* Release date: April 10, 2026

![Bodysnatcher - Hell Is Here, Hell Is Home](/img/2026Q2/hell-is-here-hell-is-home.jpg)

Sometimes I just want something stupidly heavy. This album gave me exactly that. Huge breakdowns, disgusting vocals and enough low-end to destroy whatever is nearby.

It is much more direct than most of the other albums on this list, but that is exactly why I enjoyed it. Just put it on and get crushed.

---

### Stormkeep - The Nocturnes of Iswylm

* Personal rating: 6/10
* Genre: Atmospheric / Melodic Black Metal

![Stormkeep - The Nocturnes of Iswylm](/img/2026Q2/the-nocturnes-of-iswylm.jpg)

Fast black metal riffs, huge melodies, keyboards and that cold epic sound running through the whole album.

I really liked how massive some of the songs sound. There are plenty of fast sections, but the melodic parts are what kept me coming back.
